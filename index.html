About Us

The Birhaa Garden is a local Hyderabadi restaurant in Fawkner, Victoria. We focus on traditional recipes, bold spices, and homely hospitality. Whether you dine in, take away, or order online, our aim is simple: serve soulful food that reminds you of home.
